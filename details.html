Packs

One card set. Packed for the moment.

The cards are the asset. Packaging is a toolkit: serialized card giveaway for a school visit or Science Center open house event, or a state-focused or Region-specific curated set for Capitol Hill or other specific VIPs.

📦

Center packs 6–7 cards

Each pack has 5 species cards and 1 Horizon Scanning card, and some also carry a Special Topics card. Packs go to Science Centers in waves (for example, every quarter). Every pack in a wave is identical, so it is simple to print and ship. Packs can also be handed out at the USGS booth at conferences.

🌍

Regional, state & special packs

By packaging species into regional packs or even State packs, the cards can highlight specific invaders in your backyard. We can package species by region or by state for a state delegation, or for a special purpose. Regional or State packs can also include Horizon Scanning card(s) rated high risk (4 or 5) for that area.

📋

Collector checklist & album

A roster of the full set (50+), plus the Horizon Scanning card slots. It turns a handout into a set people want to finish.

Or pack them ourselves

USGS could also print individual cards and keep them on hand so when a state visit or event comes up, staff use the By State tool below to pick the cards, slip them into a stock paperboard carrying pack (similar to what a deck of playing cards comes in) or card envelope.

  • What it takes: storage for the singles, a simple stock count, sleeves or envelopes bought in bulk, and a label printed in the office.
  • Why it helps: a state or one-off pack can be put together quickly, with no new print run.
  • Not priced yet: we have not estimated the sleeve, envelope or staff-time costs.

Where the estimates come from

Based on published 2026 trade pricing for bulk custom card printing (wjplayingcard.com, mrplayingcard.com), rephrased and recomputed. Printers did not publish foil prices at volume, so the Horizon Scanning figure is a planning assumption.

Planning tool

Know which cards matter where (by state).

Pick a state and see which invaders are here now, which species are native here, and which are on deck. Use it to brief a visit, choose cards for an event, or decide which Regional packs are worth printing. It is a working demo on the first 21 cards and will carry the full 50+.

One species, different roles. A fish can be a prized local in one state and a costly invader two states over. The cards flag where each species is invasive, native, or on deck. (See Sea Lamprey, Blue Catfish.)

Union = a card shows if it matches the state or any facet you set. Intersection = it must match the state and every facet. “Invasive here” and “On deck” are what a state pack usually leads with; toggle “Native here” to show the teaching cases too.

How this fits with the packs. Staff use this tool as a pick list: choose the cards for a state visit or event, then pack them (see “Or pack them ourselves” above). It works well for a few dozen custom sets, not thousands.
